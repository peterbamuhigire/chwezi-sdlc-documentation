# Digital marketing, social media and website strategy

Name the product, edition, accountable owner, review date and commercial decision. Record the canonical domain, repository, live state and evidence date separately. For a confidential in-house product, replace this commercial outline with a classified internal adoption plan. Use UAT evidence, authorised roles, training, controlled rollout, support and adoption measures. Exclude public campaigns, social media and public websites. Register the internal adoption document under the same Phase 01 contract.

## Buyer, alternative and proof

Identify the paying decision-maker, user, geographic market, recurring job, present workaround and purchase trigger. State a demonstrable outcome, objection and evidence for each claim. Link pricing and release status to their canonical sources. Distinguish observed facts from research hypotheses.

## Website and conversion

Audit the existing domain, redirects, entry pages, message, mobile accessibility and actual conversion path. Choose maintain, improve or build with reasons. Define site map, page purpose, evidence, search intent, language and call to action. Trace enquiry, demonstration, trial or download through fulfilment, onboarding and first value. Assign ownership for hosting, privacy, accessibility, performance and updates.

## Digital acquisition and social media

Select channels using audience behaviour, geography, trust, cost and available staff. Define search, partner, referral, email and social roles where relevant. Specify the purpose of each chosen social platform, content pillars, examples, cadence, moderation, consent, escalation and handoff. Record which channels to defer. Use evidence and permission before client stories or endorsements.

## Delivery and measurement

Define the first 90 days by hypothesis, deliverable, accountable role, resource assumption and decision date. Record funnel event, denominator, attribution limits, consent, baseline, target rationale, data owner and review cadence. Compare qualified leads, activation, retention and cost with the baseline. Record stop, change and scale criteria. Preserve unavailable baselines as unassessed.

## Acceptance and maintenance

Walk the primary journey from discovery to first value. Verify key website claims against product release evidence, review each channel with its owner and log unresolved findings. Name the evidence and next review date. After substantive review, set strategy_status to reviewed and link this source from the strategy index, PRD and export manifest.
