# Kept changes in detail

This page holds the full text of the list of kept changes that the root [`README.md`](../../../README.md) shortened on 2026-10-31, under "What the October 2026 kaizen improved". The wording is the README's, with links made relative to this page and "today" replaced by the date 2026-10-31. Scores and measures stay in the README. Each entry names its commits on `main`. "Re-run on 2026-10-31" marks a check repeated on that date on a Fedora host.

## Safety and integrity

- **Tracked settings file** (the one critical defect; K2-01). A per-user permission file, `.claude/settings.local.json`, was tracked in this public repository. It held a broad shell allowlist and local names. It is now untracked and ignored, and `scripts/check_portable_paths.py` fails on any tracked `*.local.json`. The check exited 1 on the old tree and 0 after (pass 2 experiment log); re-measured, the file is tracked at `91b987d` and absent on `main`. Still open: earlier commits hold the file, and removing it needs a history rewrite, which is the maintainer's decision. `de1efdf`
- **Package gate integrity.** The gate accepted a mismatched or symlinked plugin. An independent review on 29 October reproduced two gaps: the Claude and Codex manifests could carry different plugin names, and an active `SKILL.md` could be a symlink to a file outside the tree. A third gap, found by the root review, let the entry counter skip unreadable subtrees without an error. Each now fails, with three regression tests; the 29 October validation record reports the full suite passing with 4 skipped (453 and 455 passed in two recorded reruns). Re-run on 2026-10-31: `validate_plugin_packages.py` exits 0 with `errors: 0`. `91b987d`, `8cb2178`

## Correctness fixes

- **NFR and trace checks** (K2-04). Phase 02 and Phase 05 gave wrong answers. The NFR check passed a requirement that said "quickly" or "at all times" with no threshold, and the test-trace check rejected NFR, IR, DR and CON identifiers and module-qualified forms such as `FR-META-006`. The qualifier list, spelled-out units and identifier forms were widened. Of the 30 new test cases, 21 failed before the change and all 30 passed after; a sweep of 12 project trees and 28 NFR-pattern lines changed no verdict outside the disposable task projects. Re-run on 2026-10-31 against the old and new patterns: "quickly" and "at all times" pass before and are flagged now; `NFR-001`, `FR-META-006` and `CON-004` are rejected before and accepted now; `BG-001` is still rejected. Behaviour change: a client NFR that passed with a bare qualifier can now fail until it gains a threshold. `08dae96`
- **Clean-checkout `.docx` build** (SDLC-01). A clean checkout could not build a `.docx`, and the contents list was blank. `scripts/build-doc.sh` failed because the ignored `templates/reference.docx` was absent. The builder now generates a temporary Word template and a populated, linked contents list. On 29 October, with Pandoc 3.13, the rebuilt file opened with `python-docx` and a LibreOffice PDF showed the contents entries; a test covers the missing-Pandoc failure. Not re-run on 2026-10-31: Pandoc was absent on that host, so this was `NOT_ASSESSED` on that host. Native Word accessibility and font fidelity were never assessed. `4e5ec34`

## Portability and hygiene

- **Dev-engine references** (SDLC-06 and K2-02). Skill text pointed at dev-engine skills that do not exist at those paths. Three names were repaired on 29 October and eight more in pass 2 (the retired flat `skills/<name>` layout, or an inactive alias), and the legacy database handoff prompt and README were updated. `scripts/check_cross_engine_refs.py` now detects the class: 8 findings on the old tree and exit 0 after (9 and 0 with a local dev-engine checkout, per the pass 2 log). Re-run on 2026-10-31: exit 0. Category-path and URL resolution is `NOT_ASSESSED` without a checkout; the pass 2 record shows it passing against one. `e5698d1`, `2e5149d`, `2a02924`, `2ccbf36`
- **Machine-specific paths** (K2-03). Live files carried a Windows web-server path or a developer's home path. Eighteen files were reworded with the router's line count unchanged, and `check_portable_paths.py` fails on new ones. The historical record under `docs/` is held to a ceiling in `tests/portable-paths-baseline.json` and cannot grow. Re-measured: 44 live lines before, 0 on `main`; the check exits 0. `b8f17be`
- **First-run message and CI** (K2-08). A missing package produced a traceback, and CI skipped the new checks. `validate_skill_engine.py` and `routing_smoke_test.py` now name the package, print the install command and exit 2. The workflow gained six run steps, 12 to 18. Re-run on 2026-10-31: both scripts exit 2 with the hint. The kernel CLI and two other scripts still print a traceback (`07-docs-followups.md`, item 6). `e2e80e1`

## New checks and tests

- **Source ambiguity gate** (SDLC-04). Declared source ambiguities could not block Phase 02. A source-reconciliation register, a validator and a Phase 02 check now report each unresolved ambiguity as a blocker. On the six-ambiguity exercise the result is 6 high-severity findings and exit 1. In the pass 2 paired run the same five requirements fail with five findings when the register is present and pass when it is absent; making the register mandatory is an owner decision. Failure-path tests cover a missing or malformed register, a wrong root shape, bad source rows, identifiers and evidence, and dangling ambiguity links. The check reads declared links, not whether the register is complete. It needs the pinned packages and was not re-run on 2026-10-31. `42098e6`, `30aa407`, `293aa0a`
- **Trace evidence states** (SDLC-05). A count of design and test links said nothing about oracles, decisions or execution. A JSON schema and `scripts/validate_traceability_evidence.py` report goal, design and test links, executable oracles, open owner decisions and executed tests as separate figures. On the ten-requirement fixture they read 10 design links, 10 test links, 0 business-goal links, 3 executable oracles, 7 open decisions and 0 executed. That fixture is synthetic and is not product coverage. The validator exited 0 on 29 October and was `NOT_ASSESSED` on 2026-10-31 (`jsonschema` was absent). `6105f73`
- **Package and discovery checks** (SDLC-02 and SDLC-03). Plugin manifests, the archive ceiling and Codex discovery had no check. A repository-scoped Codex marketplace now sits beside the Claude one. `validate_plugin_packages.py` checks manifest parity for the 159 skill paths and the 5,000-entry ceiling on the full-checkout and package counts, tested at exactly 5,000 and 5,001 entries. `check_codex_discovery_summary.py` validates a sanitised offline summary. The offline Codex 0.165.0 check on 29 October found all 159 paths with `skills.max_context_tokens=10000` against 108 at the default; that counts discovered paths, not selection quality. Re-run on 2026-10-31: `validate_plugin_packages.py` exits 0, `errors: 0`. No archive was built or submitted, and the summary check was not run (no summary is held). `a596222`, `1ff245c`, `a51f164`
- **Edition drift** (K2-06). Six engine templates prescribed the 2.1 accessibility level against the engine's own WCAG 2.2 doctrine, and nothing stopped citations of superseded editions spreading. The six lines now say WCAG 2.2 AA, and `scripts/check_standards_currency.py` fails when any of eight counts rises above its committed ceiling. Re-measured on 2026-10-31: 6 lines before, 0 on `main`; the check exits 0. The ratchet does not remap clause labels, and 16 domain-pack lines wait for a jurisdiction decision. `87fa205`
- **Source review dates** (K2-07). They were prose, so an expired record raised nothing. `scripts/validate_source_register.py` fails on a record with no review date or a passed one. Pass 2 re-verified or added seven claims with source, access date and review date; where an issuer page returned HTTP 403 the record rests on a secondary check and says so. Re-run on 2026-10-31: exit 0 as of 2026-10-31, exit 1 with 3 findings as of 2026-11-30 and with 14 as of 2027-02-01, which shows it can fail. `4a48a5c`
