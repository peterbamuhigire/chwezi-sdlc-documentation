- **Uganda law:** exact titles and the known Act/Regulations source records are captured. Law currency through 2026, amendment completeness, and application to a proposed product are not asserted.

- **Model policy:** retain the pinned Luna/high instruction. Official release/catalogue records and a local cache were checked by the portfolio coordinator; this engine run has no comparative cost/latency/quality benchmark, so no policy change is supported.
- **Plugin/discovery:** official schema and budget claims are recorded above. Offline native Codex and Claude CLI installations passed.
