## Process slips and measurement corrections

1. **Stash slip.** While preparing a before-and-after comparison I ran `git stash` on the working tree by mistake. It saved every uncommitted edit; `git stash pop` restored them at once (21 changed paths before and after, stash list empty). Nothing was lost and nothing was committed from the stash.
2. **Independent measurement bug.** The baseline measurement script's flat-layout pattern excluded any name that began with a category word followed by a hyphen, so `skills/gis-mapping` was missed and the frozen baseline understates the flat-layout count by one (9 recorded; the correct count is 10 matches, of which 8 were dangling and 2 are upstream citations). The new checker found the miss; the measurement script was corrected for the re-measure. The frozen baseline file was not edited.
3. **Self-inflating counts.** After the standards ratchet was added, the unfiltered measurement showed stale-citation counts rising (IEEE 830 163 to 167, 42010:2011 13 to 19, and so on). The rise is the ratchet script and its test holding the patterns as data (37 lines). Excluding those two files reproduces 163, 13, 11, 0, 1, 1 exactly. The checker excludes them by name.
4. **Hook block.** An early `rm -rf` of a directory that did not yet exist was blocked by the destructive-command hook. It was unnecessary and was not retried or worked around.
